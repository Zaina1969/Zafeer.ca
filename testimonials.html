Women Transformed by Zafeer

Real women. Real stories. Real results from the Success Without Sacrifice program.

Xina

IT Executive

Before Zafeer She was frustrated, overworked and underpaid, struggling to spend quality time with her 3 children.
Transformation We taught Zina how to no longer be tied to the old way of working and thinking. She negotiated herself a much better paying job and a flexible working schedule. Zina is now married to her old times sweetheart; travelling, and working remotely.

Sima

Entrepreneur

Before Zafeer She was on call 24/7, doing all the work, and underpaying herself.
Transformation She stopped working harder, and started working smarter. Sima now has a powerful support team who empower her. She incorporated travel into her work — a dream for her — and she still has time for friends and family.

May

Quality Engineer

Before Zafeer She was exhausted, racing between her demanding job, her family, and her aging parents.
Transformation We taught May how to prioritize her demands, set healthy boundaries, and protect her time and mental bandwidth. Today she is in full control of her life. She was promoted to District QA Manager, and takes regular 3-day weekends with her family.

Mary

Architect

Before Zafeer She was working hard, not having enough time for fun, and still disappointed in herself for not being able to retain a job for long.
Transformation We showed Mary a new way of thinking so she stopped trying to prove herself. Mary gained confidence, started a new business, and prioritized her own needs. She is now happily married, has a beautiful baby boy, and is in full control of her own schedule.

Mar

IT Manager

Before Zafeer She was 30 and burnt out — the "go-to fixer" for her department, stressed, overwhelmed, and thinking of quitting.
Transformation We showed Mar how to re-write the rules of success to include self-care: work less and play more. Today, Mar is recognised for her advancement in Cyber Security, met the love of her life, and is planning her wedding and long honeymoon.

Noel

Retired Teacher

Before Zafeer She was 65 years old, stuck in grief from the loss of her husband, and saw no reason for living.
Transformation We showed Noel how to shift her mindset from being the victim into embracing the powerful influencer that she is. This reignited her passion for teaching. Today, Noel has her own Home School helping kids overcome learning challenges. She found a renewed purpose for life.
