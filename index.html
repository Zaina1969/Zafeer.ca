Frequently Asked
Questions

I've tried coaching before and it didn't work. Why would this be different?
Most coaching programs focus on what you need to do. Success Without Sacrifice focuses on who you need to become. The invisible ceiling — the beliefs quietly running your career decisions — is the root cause no other program addresses directly. That is the difference.
Is this program worth the investment?
Consider what one missed promotion cycle costs you — in salary, in title, in time. The investment in this program is a fraction of that. The women who complete it don't just get promoted. They stop leaving money on the table for good.
I'm already stretched thin. How will I find time for this?
One 60-minute session per week. The done-at-home exercises are designed to fit into your life — not add to the pile. And the women who feel most stretched are often the ones who need this most urgently. Being busy is not the barrier. It is the symptom.
Who is this program for?
This program is for you if you are genuinely open to seeing yourself and your situation in a new light. If you are done making excuses and ready to make serious decisions. If you appreciate 1-on-1 partnership, accountability, and guidance — rather than figuring it out alone. And if you are done running on the treadmill going nowhere and are ready to lead yourself into the next level of your career and your life.
How long is the program and what does it include?
Success Without Sacrifice is a 12-week private coaching program. Here is what you receive:

12 private 1-on-1 sessions with Zaina — one per week, 60 minutes each, over three months. Fully personalized to your situation, your organization, your invisible ceiling. No scripts. No generic playbook.

Done-at-home exercises — designed to deepen what we uncover in our sessions and anchor the shifts into your daily life.

Unlimited email support between sessions — because the moments that matter most don't always wait for a scheduled call.

Structured progress reviews at weeks 4, 8, and 12 — to ensure you're on track, adjusting course as needed, and celebrating every milestone along the way.
Is the coaching online or in-person?
All sessions are private 1-on-1 video calls between you and Zaina — there are no group classes, no cohorts, and no shared spaces. Everything you share is completely confidential. Sessions are conducted virtually, making Success Without Sacrifice accessible no matter where you are in the world, and scheduled around your calendar.
Is this program the right fit for me?
Yes — if you are a high-achieving woman in tech or corporate leadership. You excelled at your job. And yet the promotion, the title, the salary keep going to someone else.

The best way to find out is through a Discovery Call. This is a real conversation — not a sales pitch. We talk about where you are, where you want to be, and whether this program is genuinely the right fit for you right now. If it's not, Zaina will tell you.
