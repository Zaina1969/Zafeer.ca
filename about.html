Meet Zaina Warne

20 years of living it,
leading it, coaching it

With an Engineering background and a long successful career in corporate tech, I know exactly what it takes to climb to the top — and the price many women silently pay to get there.

As a first-generation professional who navigated cultural pressures, family expectations, and a relentless inner critic, I've lived the cost of succeeding on everyone else's terms. That personal reckoning is what drove me to build something bigger.

With a second degree in Psychology, and certified in both Resilience Coaching and Career Coaching, I've helped 50+ high-achieving women in tech break through what's silently holding them back — and negotiate the promotion, title, and salary they deserve.

With me, you don't just get coaching. You get a partner who understands you — and will go the extra mile with you. I built a multi-million-dollar IT consulting business using these same tools. Now it's your turn.
