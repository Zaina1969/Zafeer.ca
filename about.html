About Zaina

Zaina Warne — Biography

Coaching Built From Experience, Driven by Purpose

Zaina Warne knows what it means to sacrifice yourself for success — and she knows the moment you realise the cost is too high. A first-generation professional who navigated corporate life while managing the invisible weight of family expectations, cultural pressures, and a relentless inner critic, Zaina found herself excelling externally while quietly unravelling within. It was this personal reckoning that ignited her passion for resilience and led her to leave a high-pressure corporate career to dedicate herself entirely to coaching women.

Today, Zaina is the founder of Zafeer — a women's resilience coaching brand rooted in her deep belief that professional women should never have to choose between the life they've built and the life they deserve. With certifications in life coaching, positive psychology, and resilience facilitation, and years of experience coaching women across industries and continents, Zaina brings both the credentials and the compassion to help her clients create lasting, meaningful change.

Her approach is grounded in her proprietary D4 Formula — a four-phase coaching framework that guides women from self-discovery to sustainable transformation. What sets Zaina apart is not just her methodology, but her unwavering presence: she shows up as a coach, a champion, and a strategic thought partner who genuinely celebrates every milestone her clients achieve. With Zaina, you don't just get coaching — you get a partnership.

What I Stand For

My Values

These three values are the foundation of every coaching relationship, every session, and every breakthrough that happens inside the Zafeer space.

Empowerment

I believe every woman carries extraordinary power within her — often buried beneath doubt, expectation, and exhaustion. My role is not to give you strength; it's to help you reconnect with the strength that was always yours. Every coaching interaction is designed to expand your sense of what's possible, not limit it.

Authenticity

Real transformation requires real honesty — from me and from you. I show up in every session as my full, unguarded self, and I create a space where you can do the same. Coaching that skims the surface produces surface-level results. The Zafeer space is built for depth, truth, and the kind of clarity that only comes when you stop performing and start being.

Transformation

I am committed to change that lasts — not quick fixes or feel-good motivation that fades by Monday. Every tool, exercise, and conversation in the Zafeer program is designed to create lasting shifts in how you think, how you lead, and how you live. Transformation is not a destination. It's a practice — and I'm here to walk it with you.
