The Signature Program

Success Without Sacrifice

A 12-week resilience coaching program for professional women — built on science, fuelled by purpose, and tailored entirely to you.

Why This Program?

You Don't Have to Choose Between Success and Wellbeing

For too long, high-achieving women have been told that sacrifice is the price of success — that burning out, missing moments, and running on empty is just the cost of ambition. Zaina Warne built the Success Without Sacrifice program to prove that belief wrong.

Grounded in resilience science, positive psychology, and over a decade of coaching experience, this 12-week journey uses the proprietary D4 Formula to help you discover who you are, decide what you truly want, design a life that reflects your values, and develop the mindset and habits to sustain it.

The result? A version of you that is productive and present. Ambitious and at peace. Successful — without sacrifice.

The Framework

The D4 Formula

Four transformative phases. One powerful outcome: a resilient life built on your terms.

Phase 01 — D1

D1

Discover

Every transformation begins with honest self-awareness. In the Discover phase, we illuminate the patterns, beliefs, and behaviours that are secretly sabotaging your resilience. Through guided assessments, values mapping, and reflective exercises, you gain profound clarity on who you are and why you respond to pressure the way you do. This is the foundation everything else is built on.

Phase 02 — D2

D2

Decide

Clarity without commitment is just a daydream. In the Decide phase, you make powerful, values-aligned decisions about the life you truly want to lead — not the one you've been performing. We define your non-negotiables, your vision for success, and the boundaries required to protect what matters most. This is where your transformation becomes intentional and irreversible.

Phase 03 — D3

D3

Design

With your vision locked in, it's time to build your life architecture. In the Design phase, Zaina works with you to create a personalised resilience blueprint — a practical, sustainable plan that weaves your goals, boundaries, and self-care into your daily reality. Routines, relationships, and rhythms are all redesigned to support the woman you're becoming, not the one you've been trying to escape.

Phase 04 — D4

D4

Develop

The final phase is where everything integrates. The Develop phase focuses on building the mindset, skills, and habits that make your new life sustainable long after the program ends. You'll develop your emotional intelligence, stress-response awareness, communication boundaries, and a personalised resilience practice — so that no matter what life throws at you, you have the inner resources to meet it with grace and grit.
